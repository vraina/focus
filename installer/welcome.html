YoungWonks Focus

A focus timer that makes it visible where screen time goes. It records switches away from a task and visits to distraction sites, with a picture, so the person using the Mac and their household can see them. Nothing is ever blocked.

This installer will:

  • Put YoungWonks Focus in Applications, and start it for every person who logs in to this Mac.
  • Install a small background helper that stores records safely and sends them to your household's account.
  • Keep the app up to date automatically.

You need: an administrator password for this Mac, and a household account on the YoungWonks Focus website.
