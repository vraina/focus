Two steps left

YoungWonks Focus is installed and has opened.

  1. Allow screen recording. The app shows a button for this. Do it now, in this administrator login: it then applies to everyone who uses this Mac.
  2. Link this computer. On the website, open Computers and choose Get a code. In the app, open Account and type the code.

After that, each person who logs in to this Mac is asked who they are, or you can set it for them on the website.

For a child, use a Standard (not Administrator) Mac login, so the app and its records cannot be removed.
